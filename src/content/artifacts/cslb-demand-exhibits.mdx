## EXHIBIT A — Home Improvement Contract

Signed home improvement contract between Lisa Diaz and Sierra Crest Construction, Inc., dated January 18, 2025, for a fixed contract price of $68,500 covering a full kitchen and primary-bathroom remodel, including plumbing rough-in, electrical circuit upgrades, custom cabinetry, and tile installation, with a stated completion window of twelve weeks. Includes the required Bus. & Prof. Code § 7159 disclosures and the contractor's license number as printed on the contract face.

*Source: Client file, Contract-001 to Contract-014.*

---

## EXHIBIT B — Payment Records

Copies of cancelled checks and bank transfer confirmations showing four progress payments totaling $61,650 — 90 percent of the contract price — made by Ms. Diaz to Sierra Crest Construction between February 10, 2025 and May 12, 2025, each tied to a payment milestone represented as complete by the contractor's crew lead at the time of payment.

*Source: Client bank records, Pmt-001 to Pmt-009.*

---

## EXHIBIT C — Defect Photographs

Dated photographs taken July 2025 documenting the condition of the work site as abandoned: exposed and unterminated plumbing rough-in in the kitchen wall cavity, cabinetry installed without adequate blocking or support, and the primary bathroom stripped to the studs with no fixtures installed.

*Source: Client photographs, Photo-001 to Photo-022.*

---

## EXHIBIT D — Notice of Violation

City of Fresno Building Division notice of violation and stop-work order, dated June 10, 2025, issued following a site inspection prompted by a neighbor's report of a possible gas odor. The notice cites unpermitted plumbing work, unpermitted electrical work, and structural modifications performed without an inspection card on file.

*Source: City of Fresno Building Division, NOV-2025-04471 (FICTIONAL).*

---

## EXHIBIT E — CSLB License Detail Report

Contractors State License Board public license detail report for Sierra Crest Construction, Inc., Lic. No. 1084562 (FICTIONAL), pulled July 14, 2025, confirming the license was active throughout the contract period, identifying the contractor's $25,000 disciplinary bond and surety, and reflecting one prior CSLB complaint resolved by citation.

*Source: CSLB License Lookup, printed July 14, 2025.*

## EXHIBIT F — Independent Inspection & Corrective-Cost Report

Report of an independent licensed general contractor retained to inspect the unfinished work, dated September 2, 2025, itemizing $46,800 in corrective work required to bring the plumbing and electrical systems to code, re-secure the cabinetry, and complete the remodel to the standard contracted for.

*Source: [INSPECTING CONTRACTOR — LICENSE NO. REDACTED], Report dated 9/2/2025.*

## EXHIBIT G — Correspondence Log

Chronological log of text messages and emails between Lisa Diaz and Sierra Crest Construction, Inc. from February 3, 2025 through June 2, 2025, documenting the contractor's repeated assurances of near-completion, the final unanswered messages following the last progress payment, and the contractor's non-response to the City's notice of violation. Non-party personal contact information has been redacted.
